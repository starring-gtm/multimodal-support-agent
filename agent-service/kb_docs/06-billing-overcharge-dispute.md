# Disputing an Unexpected Charge

Customers may see charges they don't recognize for a few common reasons.

## Common causes
- **Prorated charges:** Mid-cycle plan changes are billed as a prorated amount for the remaining days of that cycle, which can look like a duplicate or unexpected charge.
- **Equipment fees:** A one-time fee may apply if a router or ONT was not returned within 14 days of a plan cancellation or downgrade.
- **Overage charges:** Some legacy plans have data caps; exceeding the cap results in an overage fee, shown as a separate line item.
- **Late payment fee:** Applied automatically if a previous invoice was not paid by its due date.

## Steps to resolve
1. Pull up the itemized invoice in the account portal under Billing > Invoice History.
2. Identify which line item the customer is disputing.
3. Cross-reference against the customer's plan change history and payment history.
4. If the charge matches a documented plan change, equipment non-return, or late payment, explain the charge with the specific date and reason.

## When to escalate
If the charge does not match any plan change, equipment status, or payment record — meaning it appears to be a genuine billing error — escalate to **Billing** for a manual invoice review and possible refund.
