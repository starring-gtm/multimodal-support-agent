# Reporting or Checking a Service Outage

## How to check for a known outage
Before troubleshooting individual equipment, check whether the issue is a broader area outage:
1. Check the NovaLink status page for the customer's region.
2. Cross-reference the customer's zip/postal code against any active incident reports.

## What counts as an outage vs. an individual issue
- **Outage:** Multiple customers in the same geographic area report loss of service simultaneously, typically correlated with scheduled maintenance or a fiber line cut.
- **Individual issue:** Only one account is affected, with no correlating reports nearby — this points to equipment or line issues specific to that customer.

## Steps if a confirmed outage is affecting the customer
1. Inform the customer of the outage, affected area, and estimated restoration time if available.
2. No further troubleshooting is needed — do not have the customer power cycle equipment during a confirmed outage, as this will not resolve anything.
3. Log the ticket as outage-related for tracking purposes.

## When to escalate
If a customer reports an outage but the status page shows no active incident for their area, and the issue persists after standard troubleshooting, escalate to **Network Operations** to investigate a possible unreported localized fault.
