# Resetting Your Account Password or Regaining Access

Use this guide if you're locked out of your NovaLink account portal or app.

## Steps to resolve
1. On the login page, select "Forgot Password."
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. A reset link is sent to that email and expires after 30 minutes.
4. If you no longer have access to that email address, account recovery requires identity verification (see below).
5. If you're not receiving the reset email:
   - Check spam/junk folders.
   - Confirm the email address on file is correct by contacting support with your account number.

## Identity verification for lost email access
If the email on file is no longer accessible, the customer must verify identity using two of the following: full name and billing address on file, last 4 digits of the payment method on file, or the account's service installation date.

## When to escalate
If a customer cannot complete identity verification through the above methods but insists the account is theirs, escalate to **Account Support** for manual review — do not reset the password without successful verification.
