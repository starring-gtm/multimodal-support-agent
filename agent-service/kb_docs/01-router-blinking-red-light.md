# Router Showing a Solid or Blinking Red Light

A red light on your NovaLink router almost always indicates a loss of signal between the router and our network, not a problem with your internal wiring.

## What the pattern means
- **Slow blinking red (1 blink per second):** The router has lost its connection to the NovaLink network. This is usually temporary and resolves within a few minutes.
- **Fast blinking red (multiple blinks per second):** The router is attempting to re-establish a connection after a drop. No action needed yet — wait 2-3 minutes.
- **Solid red (no blinking):** The router cannot detect any signal at all. This typically means either a fiber line issue on our end or a disconnected cable at your premises.

## Steps to resolve
1. Check that the fiber optic cable is firmly connected to the ONT (Optical Network Terminal) box, not just the router itself.
2. Power cycle the router: unplug it, wait 30 seconds, plug it back in.
3. If the light remains solid red after 5 minutes, check for an area outage (see "Reporting a Service Outage").
4. If no outage is reported and the light stays red, this indicates a physical line fault and requires a technician visit.

## When to escalate
If the red light persists for more than 15 minutes after a power cycle, with no reported outage in the area, escalate to **Network Operations** for a line diagnostic.
