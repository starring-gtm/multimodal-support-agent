# Cancelling Your Service

## Steps to cancel
1. Requests can be made via the account portal under Plan > Cancel Service, or by contacting support directly.
2. Service remains active until the end of the current billing cycle — no partial refunds are issued for unused days.
3. Equipment (router and ONT box) must be returned within 14 days of the cancellation date to avoid an equipment fee (see "Returning or Exchanging Equipment").

## Early termination
Customers on a promotional contract term (typically 12 or 24 months) who cancel before the term ends may be subject to an early termination fee, prorated based on months remaining. Customers on month-to-month plans have no early termination fee.

## Retention offers
Before finalizing a cancellation, support may offer a retention discount or plan adjustment if the customer's stated reason is price-related. This is optional and at the customer's discretion — cancellation must always be processed if requested, regardless of whether an offer is accepted.

## When to escalate
If a customer disputes an early termination fee, claiming they were not informed of the contract term, escalate to **Account Support** for contract record review.
