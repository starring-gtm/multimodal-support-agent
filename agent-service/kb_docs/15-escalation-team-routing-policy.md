# Escalation Policy: Which Team Handles What

When an issue cannot be resolved through standard troubleshooting steps, it should be escalated to the correct team based on the nature of the issue.

## Team ownership
- **Network Operations** — physical connectivity issues, router/ONT hardware faults, line quality issues, outages, speed/performance issues, advanced networking (static IP, port forwarding) configuration problems.
- **Billing** — payment failures, disputed charges, refunds, plan pricing questions, proration issues.
- **Account Support** — login/password/identity verification issues, cancellation requests, contract term disputes, general account access problems.
- **Logistics** — equipment shipping, returns, missing deliveries, replacement unit dispatch.

## General escalation guidelines
1. Always attempt the documented troubleshooting steps for the relevant issue category before escalating.
2. Include a clear summary of what was already tried and its outcome when escalating.
3. If an issue spans multiple categories (e.g., a billing dispute caused by an equipment return issue), escalate to the team that owns the *root cause*, not the symptom — in that example, Logistics, since the missing return is the root cause of the billing charge.
4. If the correct team is genuinely unclear after review, default to **Account Support** as the general intake point; they will re-route internally.

## Response time expectations
Network Operations and Billing escalations are typically reviewed within 4 business hours. Account Support and Logistics escalations are typically reviewed within 24 hours.
