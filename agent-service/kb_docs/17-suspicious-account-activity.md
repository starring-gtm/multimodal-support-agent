# Reporting Suspicious Account Activity

Use this guide when a customer reports logins they don't recognize, unexpected plan changes they didn't make, or suspects unauthorized access.

## Immediate steps
1. Confirm the customer's identity using standard verification (see "Resetting Your Account Password" for verification method options).
2. Once verified, immediately force a password reset on the account and invalidate all active login sessions.
3. Review the account's recent activity log (Account > Activity Log) for the customer together: login timestamps, IP addresses/locations, and any plan or billing changes.
4. If unauthorized plan changes were made, revert them and note the reversal in the ticket.
5. If unauthorized charges resulted from the activity, refer to Billing for a refund review.

## Enabling extra protection
Recommend the customer enable two-factor authentication (Account > Security > Two-Factor Authentication) to prevent recurrence.

## When to escalate
Any confirmed unauthorized access should be escalated to **Account Support** regardless of whether the immediate steps above were completed, for formal incident logging and monitoring of the account for further suspicious activity.
