# Payment Failed or Card Declined

## Common causes
- Card expired or details changed
- Insufficient funds
- Bank flagged the transaction as suspicious (common for first-time recurring charges)
- Billing address mismatch with the card issuer's records

## Steps to resolve
1. Confirm the card details on file (expiration date, billing zip/postal code) match the customer's current card.
2. Ask the customer to check with their bank if the payment was flagged or blocked.
3. Offer to retry the payment manually from the account portal under Billing > Payment Methods.
4. If retry fails again, offer an alternative payment method (a different card, or bank transfer where available).

## Grace period policy
Service is not suspended until 10 days after a failed payment. Automated reminder emails are sent on day 1, day 5, and day 9.

## When to escalate
If a customer disputes that their payment method is valid despite repeated failures, or requests a payment plan/extension beyond the standard grace period, escalate to **Billing**.
